II. What Data We Collect

1. Account Data

1.1 When you register or sign in, we process your first and last name, display name, email address, phone number (if you provide it), profile photo (if you sign in with Google), the sign-in method you use, whether your email address is verified, and the dates your account was created, updated and last used.

1.2 Passwords are handled exclusively by Google Firebase Authentication. AI Law never sees or stores your password.

2. Order Data

2.1 When you order a service, we process the services ordered, their quantity and price, your contact details, any notes you provide for the lawyer, and the status and history of your order.

2.2 Payments are processed by the bank on its own secure payment page. AI Law does not receive, see or store your card details.

3. Communications and Legal Matters

3.1 We process the information you share with us by email, phone, messengers, or through the chat on our website, as well as documents and information you provide for the purpose of a legal service.

3.2 Information relating to your legal matter is additionally protected by the attorney’s duty of professional confidentiality.

4. Data Stored in Your Browser

4.1 Our website stores a small amount of information in your browser: your chosen colour theme (light or dark), the contents of your cart, and your sign-in session. This information stays on your device and is described in Section VII.

III. Why We Use Your Data

We process personal data only where we have a lawful basis to do so:

  • To create and maintain your account and let you sign in — necessary for the agreement between you and AI Law;
  • To provide the services you order, communicate with you about them and deliver the results — necessary for the agreement;
  • To issue invoices, keep accounting records and meet other obligations imposed on us by law — legal obligation;
  • To protect the security of the website, prevent fraud and resolve disputes — our legitimate interests;
  • To send you news and offers about our services — your consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

IV. Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the following recipients, and only to the extent necessary:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Google Workspace, Google Sheets and Google Drive), which provides sign-in and stores account and order records on our behalf;
  • Banks and payment service providers, to process your payments;
  • Providers of artificial intelligence models used by our chat, when you use the chat (for example, Google, Anthropic or OpenAI), which process your messages to generate responses;
  • Courts, public authorities and other third parties, where required by law or necessary to provide the service you ordered (for example, submitting documents to a public registry on your behalf).

V. International Transfers

Some of our service providers, including Google and providers of artificial intelligence models, may store or process data on servers located outside Georgia. Where personal data is transferred abroad, we rely on the grounds and safeguards provided by the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection.

VI. How Long We Keep Your Data

6.1 Account data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account in your account settings, your sign-in credentials and your profile record are deleted.

6.2 Orders, invoices, payment and transaction records are not deleted together with your account. They are kept for as long as required by tax and accounting legislation and as needed to resolve possible disputes.

6.3 Files relating to legal matters are kept in accordance with the attorney’s professional obligations and applicable law.

6.4 Deleting your account does not by itself end an active legal engagement or outstanding contractual obligations.

VII. Cookies and Browser Storage

7.1 We use only the browser storage that is needed for the website to work: your colour theme, your cart, and your sign-in session (stored by Firebase Authentication).

7.2 We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking. If we introduce analytics in the future, we will update this Policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

7.3 You can clear this information at any time in your browser settings. Clearing it will sign you out and empty your cart.

VIII. How We Protect Your Data

We protect personal data with appropriate technical and organisational measures, including encrypted connections, verified sign-in through Firebase Authentication, and access limited to persons who need it to provide our services. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

IX. Your Rights

Under the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection, you have the right to:

  • Receive information about the processing of your personal data;
  • Access your personal data and receive a copy of it;
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Request that processing be stopped, or that your data be deleted or destroyed;
  • Request that processing of your data be restricted (blocked);
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out before the withdrawal;
  • Object to the processing of your data for direct marketing;
  • Lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority — the State Audit Office of Georgia — or with a court.

To exercise your rights, write to info@ailaw.llc. You can update your name and phone number and delete your account yourself in your account settings.

X. Age Restriction

Our services are intended for persons aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data of minors.
